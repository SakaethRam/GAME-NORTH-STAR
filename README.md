# NORTH · STAR

A fast-paced 2D arcade-style space shooter focused on responsive combat, progressive level-based gameplay, and continuous player advancement. Players navigate through star-based stages, facing increasingly challenging enemies, dynamic combat encounters, and powerful boss battles that test their abilities and progression. An integrated upgrade system allows players to strengthen weapons, abilities, and overall combat capabilities as they advance. The combination of escalating difficulty, meaningful progression, varied encounters, and replay-focused mechanics creates an engaging arcade experience designed to encourage repeated playthroughs and mastery.
